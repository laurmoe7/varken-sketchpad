# Sketchpad: detailed notes

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md`. Read this when working on `index.html`, `sketch.js`, `sketch.css` or turning an upload into game art.

- **Sketchpad** (`index.html`, `sketch.js`, `sketch.css`, `manifest.webmanifest`): a standalone page for Lauren (Windows, Chrome, a drawing tablet plugged into the PC), not part of the game; it runs in the browser (installable from Chrome as an app) and as the Windows program (see `CLAUDE.md`). It borrows the pet and toys at load by fetching `game/source.html` (so it must be served over http, not opened as a file), and uses the game snapshot in `game/` (`logic.js`, `wardrobe.js`, `skins.js`, `decor.js` and `backdrops.js`). It has its own pickers for pet, skin, outfit, backdrop and furniture (kept in `localStorage` `nibble-sketchpad`). She draws over a still copy of the pet (`Pet`: the pet drawing is 160 x 150 at 0,0 with room round it for hats; same coordinates as `wardrobe.js` and `index.html`), the stage (`Scene`: 400 x 160 backdrop with the pet box at x125 y18 w150 h140 and the toy at x295 y124), the toy (`Toy`: 26 x 26) or the room (`Furniture`: same 400 x 160 view with the pet and chosen furniture behind it; she draws one new piece, and you turn it into a `decor.js` entry: x, y = its centre / 400 and / 160, w, h in px, own view box). The page has a dim plum night theme (`body.skp` variables at the end of `sketch.css`; the drawing paper is a softer beige) with decorative fireflies in the header (`#skFlies`, CSS only; still when reduced motion is on). Tools (icon-only buttons with the shortcut letter in the corner; Grid, Pet and Ghost sit in the top bar; Images shows a Ctrl+V reminder): Pen P (thickness slider 1-40, also [ and ]; with lines picked it changes theirs), Shape G (drag a box; the shape list is `L.SKETCH_SHAPES`, outlines from `L.sketchShape` as points in a 0..1 box; Shift keeps it even; the Fill list sets Outline only, Flat, Fade down, Fade across or Glow, with a second colour for the fades; shapes are ordinary closed strokes; Filled and the bucket use the same Fill list, and a fade is stored as `grad` ({type v/h/r, c1, c2}) and written as an SVG gradient in `<defs>`; the Line list also has Soft (an airbrush: blurred by `L.sketchSoftBlur`, 70% opaque, a `<filter>` in the SVG; convert it to a gradient or a blurred shape in the game's SVG), special line styles (the Line list: dotted, tiny and wide dots, dashed, long and short dashes, dash-dot, dash-dot-dot, morse, stitches, wavy; a stroke's `style`, drawn with `L.sketchDash` in the page and the SVG so the dashes scale with the thickness; wavy bends the points once with `L.sketchWave` while drawing; picking lines and choosing a style changes them), Curve C (click to place points, drag to bend through them like a vector pen; double-click or Enter finishes, clicking the first point closes the loop, Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z removes the last point; `skCurvePts` samples the Béziers into an ordinary stroke, not tidied; the stroke keeps its points as `cv` ({closed, a: [{p, o, i?}]}) so it can be tweaked: click a curve with the Curve tool, drag a point or a handle (Alt: that handle alone), double-click the line to add a point, a point to remove it; sharp corners get handles along their lines on entry, `skXfCv` carries `cv` through move/resize/turn/flip, `skMirrored` mirrors it; with Mirror on the preview shows both halves and the two halves share a `pair` id, so tweaking one moves the other), Filled F, Bucket B (fills the area her lines close in: the lines are drawn on a hidden canvas a little thicker, `L.floodMask` spreads over the area and `L.traceLoops` outlines it, kept as a vector stroke with `bucket: true` and its own path `d` drawn with evenodd, always under the lines; an open area says so), Select S and Lasso L (click a line, drag a box over some, or draw a loop round them; Shift adds; both give the same box: corner handles resize keeping the shape (Shift: each way separately), the round handle turns (Shift: 15° steps), dragging inside moves, Delete removes, Ctrl+A picks all; `L.sketchXform` does the maths from how the lines looked when the drag began, fills carry their path `d` along; one undo step per drag), picked lines also have a row of buttons (flip left-right and up-down via `L.sketchXform` kind `flip`, duplicate Ctrl+D, copy/cut/paste Ctrl+C/X/V (`SK.clip`; the copy event leaves the text `nibble-lines` on the system clipboard so an older picture is not pasted instead), front/back of the layer, move to the active layer, delete); **layers for lines** (`SK.layers`/`SK.active` per area, saved in `nibble-sketchpad`; each stroke has `lay`; `skOrdered` sorts by layer then drawing order, hidden layers are not drawn or hit but are still exported, with `data-layer` on each path and the names in the notes; deleting a layer moves its lines down; Select, Lasso and the eraser only reach lines on the ticked layers (`SK.layerSet`, `skSelectable`): clicking a row ticks just that one, Shift/Ctrl-click or its tick box adds more, All ticks every layer; the message line `#skStatus` sits under the header); **game colours** (`skPalette` reads the main fill and outline colours from the pet, background or toy underneath as a second swatch row); the pen thickness slider has a reset button (default 9). Mirror X (a toggle, not a tool: strokes, erasing and bucket clicks are folded over the middle line, x = 80 for the pet and the middle of the view otherwise), Erase E (rubs out only the part of a pen line under it: `skEraseAt` cuts with `skEraseCut` into new lines, a loop is opened; Shift, filled shapes, ribbons and bucket fills go whole; the eraser grows with the pen thickness; the cut follows the smooth curve the page draws (`skDrawnPts`, the same quadratic curves as `L.sketchPath`), so the pieces stay exactly where the line was, and the cut ends are put exactly on the eraser's edge by `skEdgePt`; **Eraser list** (`#skEraser`, `SK.eraser`: `normal` (default), `hard`, `line`; picking one switches to the eraser). **Normal** (`skEraseNormal`, `skEraseRaster`): the eraser takes out exactly its own footprint from everything it touches (`skEraseTouches`), lines, ribbon shapes, filled shapes, scatter brush lines and bucket fills: each is drawn on a hidden canvas (a line as its stroke, a shape as its fill only, so it keeps its outline width and the eraser is widened by half of it), the eraser's whole path so far is rubbed out of it, and what is left is traced back (`L.traceLoops` like Combine shapes, but smoothed only by `skSoftenCorners`, a small length-limited corner rounding: `L.fitCurve` bulges by units on a long thin shape with few points; the loops then get evenly spaced points so the clean export's own curve fit has enough to hold on to) into a `d` shape with holes where needed (one loop also gets exact `cv`; `pts` is the loop with the biggest box, the outside); the lines go back to the snapshot taken when the pointer went down on every move and the whole path is applied again, so nothing drifts from being traced over and over; dashed and soft lines are cut as lines. **Clean cut** (`hard`): a cut plain line becomes a **ribbon shape** (`skLineToRib`: a filled polygon from `skRibPoly`, with `rib` = its middle line `pts`, a width per point `w`, the thin outline `t`, the caps `cap0`/`cap1` and for a cut end the points of its cut face, `face0`/`face1` from `skCutFace`: where each fibre of the ribbon meets the eraser's edge, so the cut has the eraser's own shape, a round bite or the ruler's straight line) so the cut is clean and hard; ribbon shapes (every pressure line and ink-brush line carries `rib` from the start) are cut again along their middle line by `skEraseRib`, hard or round as the tick says, and `skXfRib` moves the middle line and the faces along with every move, turn, flip, resize, copy, Spin, Repeat, crop, stretch and mirror; dashed, soft, filled, scatter-brush and bucket shapes still go whole; with Hard cuts off a cut line stays a line with round ends; every ribbon shape also carries its outline as exact curve anchors (`cv` from `skRibCv`, the same curves the page draws through the polygon), so the clean export writes it as it is instead of fitting new curves, which made thin ribbons wobble (the Curve tool leaves ribbons alone); **Ctrl-drag is a ruler** (`skDrawing.straight`, `skEraseStraight`): the lines go back to how they were before the press on every move and the exact straight band from the press point to the pointer, `skSegDist` wide, is rubbed out again, with a dashed guide line; lifting drops an undo step that changed nothing, Esc puts the lines back), Eyedropper I (the browser's `EyeDropper`, picks from anywhere on the screen; where the browser has no `EyeDropper` (Firefox) it becomes a click-on-the-picture tool instead: `skSampleAt` reads the colour of the topmost stroke, picture layer or pet/background shape under the pointer), Pan H (or hold Space and drag, or the middle button), Move image M (with no image on the canvas it moves every line on the ticked layers), Undo Ctrl+Z, Redo Ctrl+Y; the mouse wheel zooms towards the pointer. Pictures to trace are **layers** (`SK.images`, per area, in the area's coordinates; added with the button, by pasting an image (Ctrl+V) or dragging a file onto the canvas; opacity, size, rotation, behind or over the pet, show/remove; the dashed box shows only while the Move image tool is on; kept in `localStorage` `nibble-sketchpad-img`) and are never part of the exported SVG. Strokes are vectors in those coordinates; each is tidied when she lifts the pen (`L.tidyStroke`: smoothing, straight lines, ovals; she can switch it off or pick Faint, Subtle, Light, Normal or Strong), and `L.sketchSvg` writes them as an SVG (the notes, tidy setting and pet state are in its first comment). **The tools sit in a bar left of the canvas (`.skp-toolbar`); the panel has four pages** (tabs: Draw (picked-lines buttons, colours, pen settings, Layers, Images), Pet or Room (the picture underneath; hidden for the toy), Send, Sent; `skTab`; the drop-down menus are the game's `app-select.js` styled for the night theme; the note for Claude is a big `textarea`). **Clean curves** (on by default, `SK.clean`): the saved SVG fits a few cubic Béziers to each line with `L.fitCurve` (Schneider's algorithm, corners kept; `L.sketchPathClean`), writes curves made with the Curve tool exactly from their own points, and each layer becomes a named `<g id data-layer>` group; off keeps every pen point. **Game item code**: for a pet drawing of a hat, clothes, glasses, mouth thing, neckwear or shoes, the Send page's name box and `Copy item code` (or Upload, which adds `<name>.item.js` beside the SVG) make a ready-to-paste entry for the list in `wardrobe.js` (`L.sketchItemCode`: id, slot, label, icon box, placeholder hover lines, svg with colours and widths on each part so it needs no CSS; gradient and blur ids are prefixed with the item id); when she uploads one, check the entry, add real hover lines, and paste it into `wardrobe.js` (the pet drawing coordinates are the same, so it sits where she drew it; remember the game has no 'behind the pet' layer for hats). **Newer drawing aids**: Outline (`skOutline`: picked closed or filled shapes get a closed line above them, in the colour, Thickness and Position set in the Outline row (`#skOlColor`, `#skOlW`, `#skOlPos`); Position moves the edge evenly outwards or inwards (an inline) by drawing the shape on a hidden canvas, `L.offsetMask` growing or shrinking it, then tracing and smoothing the new edge), Make it a curve (`skToCurve`: `L.fitCurve` points become `cv` so the Curve tool can edit a pen line), Fill patterns (Stripes, Dots, Checks, Hearts, Stars: a `grad` with type from `L.SKETCH_PATTERNS` and tile size `s` = a sixteenth of the picture, written as an SVG `<pattern>` in `userSpaceOnUse`, so it stays put when the shape moves), Clip K (pet only: new lines get `clip: true` and are cut off at the generic body outline `L.SKETCH_BODY_PATH`, a mask (white body, black 2.6 edge like the game's outline) so lines stop flush with the inside of the outline, written as `<mask id="bodyclip">`; it is the same body for every species and does not apply to lines already drawn; the clipboard is `SK.clip`, the toggle is `SK.bodyClip`), Spin R (`SK.radial` 0/3/4/5/6/8: every new line, and its mirror, is copied turned round the middle with `skSpin`; copies are plain lines, curve copies lose their points; eraser and bucket use `skSpots`), and Pressure (a drawing tablet pen, `pointerType === 'pen'`, on the Pen tool with a solid line: the line swells with `e.pressure` and is tidied like any line with the pressures carried over, then saved as a ribbon shape from `skRibPoly` with its middle line in `rib`). Smooth (`skSmooth`: refits each picked pen line with `L.fitCurve` and keeps plain points, each press smoother), Join gaps (`skJoin`: picked open ends within 5% of the view meet in the middle; a line whose own ends meet is closed) and Same style (`skPaintToggle`, `SK.paint`: copies the first picked line's colour, width, style and fill, then every line clicked with Select is painted until the button or Esc). Repeat along a line (`skRepeat`: the longest picked line is the path, the other picked lines are copied along it `#skRepN` times, turned with the line unless `#skRepTurn` is off, as if drawn on a flat line going right; the single shape is used up, undo brings it back). Pick object (`skCutAi`, replaced the earlier RMBG-1.4 Cut out subject: SlimSAM, a small Segment Anything, `Xenova/slimsam-77-uniform` through transformers.js 3.0.2, loaded by `skAiLoad` from jsDelivr and Hugging Face the first time and then kept by the browser; pick mode `skCutSess` reads the picture once, then each click `skCutPoint` adds a point (Alt: a point to leave out) and everything outside the best mask goes see-through, always cut from the original pixels; not tested in the sandbox, which blocks those hosts, so the tensor shapes follow the library's SAM example) and Plain background (the colour method, `skCut`, Images box: `L.cutBackground` spreads in from the edges over colours within the Strength of a corner colour and makes them see-through (edge pixels half), the picture layer's `src` becomes the new PNG, `skCutOrig` keeps the original for Undo cut until the page closes; colour-based only, no subject detection). To shapes (`skTrace`, the "✦ To shapes" button; it was called Trace until the rename, and its layer is now "Shapes" in the Images box: the selected picture is drawn at working size, then `L.traceInk` (Otsu split plus the Ink slider) or `L.traceColours` (k-means, `L.traceSmoothLabels`; see-through pixels, such as outside the picture or after Cut out background, are not traced; each colour's shape also covers every smaller colour above it, drawn biggest first, so a dropped speck shows the colour beneath, not a hole; thin rims are dropped) gives masks, `L.despeckle` removes specks, `L.traceParts` splits them into patches, `L.traceLoops` outlines each, and `L.fitCurve` smooths them (a curve that strays from its outline falls back to the plain outline); each patch is a filled `d` shape (not a bucket fill) on a new layer called Trace, at most 600 (the biggest across all colours), and the picture is hidden afterwards). **Combine shapes** (picked closed shapes: join, cut out of the lowest, keep the overlap; drawn on a hidden canvas, combined and traced like the bucket, then smoothed with `L.fitCurve`; `skCombine`). **Sent page** (owner-only tab): lists `drawings/` on the drawings branch through the GitHub API (grouped by name: `.svg`, `.strokes.json`, `.item.js`), newest first, with thumbnails; Show puts the SVG over the picture as an image layer, Edit opens the original lines (`.strokes.json`, written by Upload from `skOriginal`: the lines in drawing order with their layers and the pet state; older uploads have none) and Undo brings back what was there, ✕ deletes the files from GitHub. On a new browser the Send page has an "Owner? Turn on uploads" link (`#skOwnerLink`) that does what `?owner=1` does. The Upload button and the GitHub connection box are hidden for visitors (`body.skp-guest`, `.owner-only`): they show only in a browser that has a token saved or has been opened once with `index.html?owner=1` (`nibble-sketchpad-owner`); this only keeps the button out of the way, the real lock is that uploading needs her token. "Upload to Claude" puts `drawings/<kind>-<mode>-<time>.svg` (plus `.strokes.json`, and `.item.js` when an item name is given) on the **`drawings` branch** of the repo through the GitHub API with a fine-grained token she pastes in once (kept in her browser only); the branch keeps main (and its Pages deploy) untouched. When she says she uploaded something: read it with `get_file_contents` on `drawings/` at ref `drawings`, redraw it cleanly as game-style SVG (not the raw strokes) and compare it over the pet. Save SVG and Copy SVG are the fallbacks. It goes live with the rest of the site on a big push.

- Sketchpad layout (since the interface review): the buttons for picked lines are no longer in the Draw panel; they sit in `#skPickBar`, a floating bar inside `main.skp-canvas` that `skPickBarPlace` puts above the picked lines (below if there is no room) whenever lines are picked with Select or Lasso, in three labelled groups (Arrange, Shapes, Clean up). Outline and Repeat open `#skOlPop` / `#skRepPop` (`skPop`) with their settings and an apply button. Images, Trace and Cut out live in the folding `#skPics` box ("Pictures"), which opens when the first picture is added. `#skRing` is a ring the size of the pen or eraser. Layers made by a tool (Trace) are in `skAutoLayers`: Undo drops them when empty and Redo brings them back (`skFixAutoLayers`).
 The Undo trace button (`skTraceUndo`) removes the latest trace's shapes and layer and shows the picture again (`skTraceLast`; Ctrl+Z does the same through `skFixAutoLayers`, and Redo hides the picture again).
 The free Canvas (`SK.mode` 'canvas', view `SK_VIEW.canvas`, default 800 x 600, saved in `nibble-sketchpad` as `canvas`): nothing underneath, no Pet tab, no Pet/Ghost controls. `+ New canvas` and `Resize` sit right of the title (`#skNewCanvas`, `#skResize`; Resize only while on the Canvas) and open `#skCanvasPop` (presets, width and height 16-4000, and for Resize an optional 'stretch the drawing' that scales strokes and pictures from the top-left); there is one free canvas at a time, a new one replaces it after a confirm, and Resize clears its undo history. `skSetMode` switches drawing areas.
 Lazy brush (`SK.lazy`, `SK.lazyN` 1-100; toggle `#skLazy` next to the Pen tool, key Z, strength slider `#skLazyRow` under the thickness slider): while drawing with the Pen or Filled tool the line follows a point pulled on a string of length `v.w * 0.0008 * lazyN` (`skDrawing.brush` in `skMove`); the line stops where the string is, with no catch-up point. Pictures: with the Move image tool a click on a picture picks it and drags it (`skImageAt`), a click on empty space lets go of it, and with nothing picked a drag moves the lines; a new picture is picked and the Move image tool is switched on.

- **History box** (`#skHist`, a folding box under Pictures; `skHistLog`, `skHistGo`, `skStepName`): lists the undo steps of the open canvas, newest first, from Start; the step you are on is pink, steps that Redo could bring back are faint and italic. A step's name is worked out from the two states it sits between (lines added, removed or changed; `skStepName`), since the history only holds snapshots of the lines. Clicking a step rebuilds `SK.hist`/`SK.redo` around it (the same as that many Undo or Redo presses). The list is redrawn a moment after each change (`skHistSoon`, called from `skRedraw`, `skHistoryUI` and `skSetMode`) and only while the box is open. - **Brushes** (the Brush list `#skBrush` in the Draw panel, `SK.brush`, saved in `nibble-sketchpad`; the list `SK_BRUSHES` has id, name and the status line): pen brushes after the well-known Clip Studio Paint ones, for the Pen tool only (picking one switches to the Pen). Plain pen is the ordinary line. The others always read the pen pressure (`s.pr`, a mouse presses evenly) and skip the Line list and the Pressure tick; they only change new lines. Tidy works on them: the middle line is tidied first and the pressures are carried over by distance along it (`skRemapPr`). When the pen lifts (and live while drawing, `skLiveStroke`), `skBrushStroke` turns the points and pressures into **one filled shape**, so `L.sketchSvg` and the game need nothing new: the ink pens are a ribbon polygon in `pts` from `skBrushPoly` (widths per point, caps round, flat or frayed, edge jitter and nicks; `skTaper` thins the ends, `skSmoothPts` steadies the middle line) and the scatter brushes (Spray, Sparkles, Hearts, Bubbles: `skStampStroke`) are many small loops in `d` drawn evenodd like a bucket fill (a bubble is a ring: outer loop, hole, shine), placed along the line without overlapping (a grid of squares keeps it quick), with `pts` the line itself for picking. The ink pens first spread the middle line into even steps (`skResampleP`, 0.35 of the pen width apart) so a tidied line of few points comes out the same as a slow dense one. G-pen swells with pressure and tapers to points; Mapping pen is a thin version; Brush pen is fat where slow or pressed and thin where flicked; Calligraphy is a nib at 45° (width = how much of the nib crosses the direction), flat ends; Marker is even with flat ends; Pencil is thin with a grainy edge; Rough pen has ragged edges and nicks; Bristle brush has frayed ends; Watercolour is a wide ribbon with `style: 'soft'` and `width` 0.7 x the pen, which sets its blur. Random bits come from `skRng` seeded by the first point, so the preview matches the saved line. The fill's own outline is thin (`v.w * 0.0015`), like a pressure line. A brush line is a fill to everything else (the eraser cuts it along its middle line, see Erase): Smooth, Make it a curve and Join gaps skip it, Select moves and resizes it (`d` is carried along, and `skMirrored` mirrors `d` too). With Clean curves on, the saved SVG fits curves to each loop, which smooths the Pencil's grain and rounds the smallest spray dots a little.
- **Settings and About:** Edit > Settings… (the old Preferences) opens `#skPrefsDlg` with two tabs, Preferences and About Varken Sketchpad (`skPrefTab`, `skAboutFill`); Help > About opens the same box on the About tab.
